About Us

PT. Guna Cipta Sinergi was established in September 2019 as an expansion of PT Guna Cahaya Synergie, which was founded in September 2006, originally to serve Service & Repair operations for several multinational companies.

Through effort and hard work, the aim is to become a company that contributes to Indonesia’s development and construction into a more advanced nation. The company has served customer needs for IT-based ATM services and supporting devices across Indonesia for more than 6 years.

🏢 Established 2019

Company History

A brief timeline of the company since its establishment.

2006

Beginning

PT Guna Cahaya Synergie was founded in September 2006 with a focus on providing information technology solutions for local businesses. The journey began with a small team serving MSME clients in Jakarta.

2019

Service Expansion

In 2019, the company expanded its services to cloud solutions, system integration, and 24/7 support. We began serving corporate clients and strengthened our development and customer service teams.

2022

Productive Focus

We sharpen our focus on SaaS products and business process automation modules. R&D investment is increasing to deliver faster and more reliable features for users.

2025

Present & Vision

Until 2025 we continue to innovate, expand regional reach, and remain committed to providing reliable solutions that support clients’ digital transformation across various sectors.
